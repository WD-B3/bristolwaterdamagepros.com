About Bristol Water Damage Pros

A Bristol-based dispatch line and crew for water damage in the East Bay.

Emergency Dispatch

Call Now

Who we are

Bristol Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration dispatch and crew service based at 576 Metacom Ave in Bristol, Rhode Island. We handle emergency water removal, drying and repair for homes and businesses in Bristol, Warren, Barrington, Portsmouth and Tiverton. We are licensed and insured.

Our dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day. A coordinator asks what happened, tells you what to shut off and sends a crew when one is free. Same-day response is available when a crew is open, and we do not promise a specific arrival time, since storms and roads around the bay change it.

How we work

We explain scope and price before work starts, take moisture readings and keep a log you can give your insurer. We work to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage and S520 for mold. We do not promise that any insurance claim will be approved.

  • Stop the source and make the area safe.
  • Remove water and unsalvageable materials.
  • Dry the structure to normal readings.
  • Repair what the water ruined.

Where to find us

Visit our contact page for the address and map, or see the full list of services and the towns we cover.

What we handle

Our work starts with water. We handle burst pipes, floods, sewage backups, storm damage, appliance and water heater leaks, wet basements and drying after any of them. Mold remediation follows when water has lingered. The full list is on the services page, and each service has its own page that explains the cause, the first steps and the cleanup.

We work in homes, apartment buildings, offices and shops. Commercial jobs are planned around downtime so a business can stay open where possible.

What you can expect when you call

A coordinator answers the dispatch line at any hour. You describe what happened, and they tell you what to shut off and what not to touch. If a crew is free, it is sent. If not, the coordinator tells you plainly and explains what to do in the meantime.

When the crew arrives, they take moisture readings and explain what they see. You get the scope and the price before work starts, and you can ask questions. If the scope changes, you hear about it and approve it first.

How we document

We record moisture readings, photograph affected areas and keep a log of the equipment placed and the work performed. You can give those records to your insurer. We do not decide coverage and we do not promise that any claim will be approved.

Standards we follow

Water damage work follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for professional water damage restoration. Mold work follows ANSI/IICRC S520. Those standards define water categories, drying goals and containment practices, and they give you something to check a company against.

The towns we serve

Our base is in Bristol, Rhode Island, and we serve Warren, Barrington, Portsmouth and Tiverton from here. Each has its own page with the local building department, water supplier and storm history that matter to a homeowner.

What sets a good restoration job apart

A good job is measured, documented and explained. Moisture readings, not appearances, decide when the structure is dry. A written scope tells you what will be done. And a coordinator who answers the phone at midnight tells you what to do before the crew arrives.

We aim to make each of those things true on every call.

Licensed and insured

We are licensed and insured. If you need proof for a landlord, a condominium board or an insurer, ask when you call, and we will explain what we can provide.

Our approach to communication

Water damage is stressful, and plain talk helps. We explain what we see, what we plan to do and what it is likely to involve. If something changes, we tell you before we act. If we do not know, we say so.

You can call dispatch with a question at any point, including after the crew has left.

Why we are based in Bristol

Bristol sits on a peninsula between Narragansett Bay and Mount Hope Bay. A crew based here knows the roads and bridges, the older housing and the weather that comes up the bay. That local knowledge shapes how we plan each job.

If you have never needed restoration before, you are not alone. Most people call us in the middle of a bad day, and we try to make the next hour easier.

If you have a question about how we work, call dispatch and ask. We would rather answer before a job than after it.

We also invite you to read the guides on this site. They explain water damage, drying and insurance in plain language, and they are written to help you decide what to do next, whether or not you hire us.
