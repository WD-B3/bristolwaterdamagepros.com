Frozen pipe water damage is water released when a pipe that froze and split begins to thaw. The pipe may have been cracked for days before anyone notices, because the ice plug keeps the water in until the temperature rises.

This page is about the freeze as the cause. A pressurized line that fails for another reason belongs under burst pipe damage. How often pipes freeze in your own house depends on its insulation and exposure, so we describe the mechanism here rather than a local frequency.

Frozen pipe emergency: what to do during a thaw

If a faucet runs slowly or not at all in cold weather, a pipe may be frozen. Keep the faucet open, shut off the main water valve and leave the pipe alone. Do not use a torch or open flame, since that is a common way to start a fire in a wall.

Warm the area gently with a hair dryer or space heater placed safely away from anything flammable, working from the faucet toward the frozen section. If you see water, hear dripping in a wall or find a wet ceiling, shut off the main and call dispatch. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

Where pipes freeze in a Rhode Island house

Pipes freeze where cold air reaches them: along exterior walls, in unheated garages and crawl spaces, in attics and in short runs that pass through an uninsulated rim joist. Older shingled houses with thin wall cavities and homes with a kitchen or bath on an outside wall are the usual suspects.

New England winters bring deep cold snaps, and a house left cooler while owners travel is more vulnerable. Keeping the heat on, opening cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and letting a faucet drip during a hard freeze are the standard protections. See crawl space water damage if the line that split runs under the house.

Cleanup after a pipe has split

The restoration order is the same as any pressurized line failure. Stop the supply, extract standing water, map moisture and dry. A split pipe in a wall often wets the insulation and the back of the drywall, and cold wall cavities dry slowly, so readings matter more than appearance.

  • Shut-off and drain-down so the line stops leaking.
  • Extraction of standing water and wet pad.
  • Moisture mapping of exterior walls, ceilings and subfloor.
  • Heated, dehumidified drying until readings are normal.

Water that came through the ceiling follows ceiling water damage repair, and the equipment stage is described under structural drying.

Common questions about frozen pipe water damage in Bristol

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Gentle heat from a hair dryer is the safe method. Never use an open flame, and shut off the main if you see any leak, because the pipe may already be split.

Why did the leak start after the freeze ended?

Ice can hold a split pipe closed. When it melts, the water flows out through the crack, so the damage often appears hours after the cold breaks.

Is insulation behind the wall wet?

Often yes. A moisture meter and thermal camera show whether insulation is holding water, which decides whether the wall must be opened.
