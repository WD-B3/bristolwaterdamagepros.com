Island water districts and permits

The Portsmouth Water and Fire District serves nearly all of Portsmouth, with the southwest corner served by Newport Water. The district's phone is (401) 683-2090. Repairs that need permits go through the Portsmouth Building Inspection office at 2200 East Main Road, (401) 683-3611.

If your property is on the shoreline, check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address before you decide on rebuild plans.

Insurance and flood coverage on the island

Flood is not covered by a standard homeowners policy, and flood coverage usually has a 30-day waiting period, per the Rhode Island Division of Insurance. The Division's phone is (401) 462-9520. Take photos before cleanup and keep receipts. No claim outcome is promised.

How a Portsmouth job usually runs

Call dispatch with your address and a short description. The coordinator explains what to shut off and how a crew would reach you, since the route to the island matters. A crew arrives, checks safety, takes readings and explains scope and price before starting.

Typical steps are extraction, removal of wet materials that cannot be saved, drying with air movers and dehumidifiers and monitoring until readings are normal. Repairs follow once the structure is dry.

Shoreline homes and seasonal properties

Homes close to the water and seasonal properties that sit empty for part of the year face a particular risk. A pipe that bursts in an unheated house can run for days before anyone arrives. If you have a seasonal home, ask a neighbor or property manager to check it after cold snaps and storms.

Consider closing the main valve and draining the system when a house will be empty for a long period. It is a simple step that prevents some of the costliest losses.

Storm preparation for Aquidneck Island homes

Before a storm, clear gutters and downspouts, check sump pumps and move valuables off basement floors. After a storm, photograph the damage before anything is moved. Dispatch is answered around the clock if water comes in during the night.

Ask the Portsmouth Building Inspection office which permits apply to your repair before work begins, since requirements depend on the scope of the work.

Tell dispatch if your property is gated, on a private road or hard to find, since access can slow a crew.
