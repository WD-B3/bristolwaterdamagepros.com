Where The Narrows sits

A real-estate neighborhood guide describes The Narrows as an east-side Bristol suburb along Mount Hope Bay, within ZIP code 02809. It notes wide, grid-patterned streets and a mix of raised ranches, ranches, Colonials, Cape Cods and split-levels. The name is a local label and not a legal boundary.

Mount Hope Bay lies on the east side of town. Homes that back onto the bay face wind and weather from that direction, and homes set back on the grid streets deal mostly with rain and drainage.

Raised ranches, split-levels and lower levels

A lower level that sits partly below grade is cooler and damper than the floors above it. When it is finished with carpet, drywall and trim, a small amount of water can reach a lot of material. The carpet water damage restoration page explains how pad and carpet are handled.

Walls take water up from the floor like a wick. A crew checks drywall and baseboards above the visible line, since moisture often travels higher than the stain. See wall and drywall water damage repair for how wet sections are cut and dried.

Bay storms and heavy rain

The National Weather Service records that Hurricane Carol in 1954 sent a storm surge of 10 to 15 feet across Rhode Island. Bristol sits between two bays, so a storm can affect either side of town. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

After a storm, check windows, doors and the roof for leaks before you check the basement. Water that comes in at the top often runs down inside a wall and shows up at the bottom. Report any standing water in a lower level to dispatch right away.

Before We Arrive in The Narrows

Stop the water

Close the main valve or the appliance valve. Tell dispatch if you cannot reach it.

Stay out of wet lower levels

Do not walk into water over the floor if outlets or cords are submerged.

Raise what you can

Lift boxes and small furniture onto upper shelves or a dry floor above.

Take photos

Record the water line on the walls and the damaged contents.

Sump pumps, window wells and grading

Pour water into the sump pit until the float lifts the pump and watch the discharge. Make sure the discharge line is not frozen or clogged. Clear window wells of leaves and snow so water does not pour against the glass.

If your lower level keeps taking water, cleanup alone will not stop it. A waterproofing contractor can address the cause, while a basement water damage cleanup removes the water and dries what was wet.

Common questions from The Narrows

Is The Narrows in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Search your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center or ask your insurer.

Why does my lower level smell damp after a storm?

Moisture can linger in carpet, pad and drywall even after the visible water is gone. A moisture reading tells whether the materials are dry.

Can I save carpet in a finished lower level?

Carpet that got only clean water and is dried quickly may be saved, but pad is usually replaced. Water from a drain or the bay means the carpet is discarded.

Do you cover bay-facing homes?

Yes. Call dispatch and describe where the water entered. The crew will check safety, take readings and explain scope and price before work begins.
