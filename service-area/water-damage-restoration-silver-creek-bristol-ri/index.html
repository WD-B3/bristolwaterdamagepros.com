Water damage near Silver Creek: what the record shows

Creek overflow

East Bay RI reported that Silver Creek overflowed its banks and blocked Hope Street and Chestnut Street after the remnants of Hurricane Ida on September 2, 2021.

Drainage capacity

A drainage study by the engineering firm BETA, hired by the town, found capacity deficiencies in the drainage network leading to flooding in the watershed.

Tides and rain together

A May 2024 report on Route 114 flooding said the road floods from a combination of high tides, high winds and regular rainfall.

Sewer pressure

In 2021 East Bay RI reported untreated water coming up from manholes where Hope Street and Ferry Road meet.

What the public record says about Silver Creek

BETA describes Silver Creek as a tidal water body. The Town of Bristol hired the firm to find the root causes of flooding and to propose fixes for 2-year, 10-year and 100-year storms. The study also found that tides from Bristol Harbor reduce the creek's capacity.

East Bay RI reported on May 8, 2024 that the creek now regularly spills over near Hope and Washington Streets and causes major traffic disruptions during rain events. Bristol, Warren and Barrington have joined the state to look at Route 114 flooding.

A resident letter in the same paper described creek water spilling over the lawn below the Guiteras School and covering a long stretch of the Hope Street bike path. A letter is one person's account, so treat it as a description and not a measurement.

What creek and storm water does inside a building

Creek and storm water carries mud, road runoff and sometimes sewage. Under ANSI/IICRC S500, floodwater is handled as contaminated, so carpet, pad and wet drywall that touched it are usually removed and not dried in place. Hard surfaces can be cleaned and disinfected.

If water comes up through a floor drain or toilet, treat it as contaminated and keep children and pets away. The sewage backup cleanup page explains what a crew does in that case.

Before We Arrive near Silver Creek

Stay out of standing water

Do not wade through water that touches outlets, cords or appliances. Call dispatch from a dry spot.

Keep power off wet areas

Do not touch the panel while standing in water. If you cannot reach it dry, say so on the call.

Lift what you can

Move small items, rugs and boxes to shelves or upper floors if the path is dry.

Note the water line

Photograph it on the wall with a ruler or a household object for scale.

Basements, drains and sump pumps

A Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management document describes wet-weather sewer overflows and names sump pumps tied into the sewer as a major cause. If your sump pump discharges into a sanitary drain, ask a plumber or the town whether that connection is allowed.

Run water into the pit before heavy rain and listen for the pump to start. A battery backup helps when power fails during a storm. A basement water cleanup starts with extraction, then removal of wet materials that cannot be saved, then drying.

Roads, access and arrival

When the creek spills, Hope Street can close and traffic is diverted. A crew may need another route, and that can change the arrival time. We do not promise a set arrival time. Tell dispatch your cross street and which nearby roads are under water.

Flood coverage and your policy

Water that rises from a creek or a tide is flood, and the Rhode Island Division of Insurance says homeowners policies do not cover flood. A separate flood policy usually has a 30-day waiting period, so it has to be in place before a storm. Ask your insurer how your loss is classified.

Common questions from Silver Creek

Does Silver Creek flood?

East Bay RI reported creek overflow onto Hope Street in September 2021 and regular spill-over near Hope and Washington Streets by May 2024. Whether one property floods depends on its elevation and drainage.

Is my Silver Creek home in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Search your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, since zones can differ from lot to lot.

Is creek water safe to clean up myself?

Creek and storm water can carry sewage and chemicals. Stay out of standing water that touches outlets, wear boots and gloves for wet items and call dispatch before moving anything saturated.

What if Hope Street is closed when I call?

Tell the coordinator your cross street and which roads near you are under water. The crew will plan the safest route, though we cannot promise a specific arrival time.
